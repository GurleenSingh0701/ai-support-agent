# Autodesk Customer Support & Store Policies

## Shipping Policy
Standard orders ship within 1-2 business days. Express shipping guarantees 24-48 hour delivery. Tracking numbers are emailed upon order dispatch.

## 30-Day Return & Refund Policy
Items in original condition can be returned within 30 days of purchase for a full refund to the original payment method. Prepaid return labels are provided free of charge for damaged or incorrect items.

## Warranty & Technical Support
All hardware products include a 1-year limited manufacturer warranty. Software subscriptions include 24/7 technical customer support.

## Escalation Policy
If an issue cannot be resolved by our automated assistant or involves custom account adjustments, tickets are escalated to a human Support Agent within 15 minutes.
